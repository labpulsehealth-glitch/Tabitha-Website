Nigerian Services Platform

NaijaCraftLink

Connect with skilled hands you can trust.

THE PROBLEM

Finding a good local professional shouldn't be so hard.

There are thousands of skilled people in Nigeria who can do excellent work — electricians, tailors, photographers, technicians, cleaners, artisans and many others.

But finding the right person can still depend on asking around, relying on someone's contact, checking WhatsApp statuses or simply hoping that the person you found is good.

THE IDEA

Put the right people in one place.

NaijaCraftLink is a platform concept that allows customers to discover skilled professionals based on their profession, location, availability, ratings, prices and previous work.

Professionals can create profiles and showcase what they can do.

WHAT I BUILT

From an idea to a working MVP.

I designed and built a mobile-first MVP that demonstrates how the platform could work.

Users can search for professionals, filter results, view professional profiles, explore portfolios and request services.

Professionals can also go through a registration flow and access a demonstration dashboard.

CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

  • 01 Search for professionals
  • 02 Filter by location and service
  • 03 View profiles and portfolios
  • 04 Compare ratings and prices
  • 05 Request a service

PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE

  • 01 Create a professional profile
  • 02 Add skills and services
  • 03 Showcase previous work
  • 04 Manage service requests
  • 05 Track availability

WHAT I LEARNED

A pretty interface isn't the same as a solution.

This project taught me to think beyond the appearance of a website.

I had to think about what happens when someone searches, what information they need before trusting a professional, what happens when there are no results and what the professional experiences on the other side.

It helped me understand product thinking a little more.
