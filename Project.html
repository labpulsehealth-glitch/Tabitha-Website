HealthTech

LabPulse

Your Digital Health Companion.

THE IDEA

What if finding healthcare information was easier?

LabPulse is a digital health platform concept designed to make some parts of healthcare easier to navigate.

The idea is to help people find laboratories, compare test prices, keep medical records and understand their laboratory results more easily.

WHAT I BUILT

  • 01 Laboratory discovery
  • 02 Test price comparison
  • 03 Medical record storage
  • 04 Result interpretation concept
  • 05 Test booking concept

WHAT I LEARNED

Building something is different from imagining it.

LabPulse taught me that an idea can look very simple when it is in your head and become much more complicated when you actually try to build it.

It made me more interested in solving real problems rather than simply making websites that look nice.
